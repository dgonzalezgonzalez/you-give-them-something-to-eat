## Sorted-threshold quota calculation

For a uniform k-subset, the average exponential of `(n/k)sum_selected(x)-sum_all(x)` is symmetric and convex in x. Convexity reduces the box x_j∈[0,W_j] to vertices. At a global vertex maximum, fix the number m of active coordinates. If an active W_i is smaller than an inactive W_j, remove the active value and write the symmetric convex scalar function g(s). Global maximality gives g(W_i)≥g(0); convex secants give g(W_j)≥g(W_i). Swapping preserves a maximum. Thus the m largest weights can be active, for each m=0,…,n and both signs. The u=1 endpoint reduces to the other sign by complementation.

For each resulting population let a_j=exp{s λ(nx_j-S)/k}. The quota average is the elementary symmetric mean, evaluated by the positive recurrence

`D[t,r]=(t-r)/t D[t-1,r]+(r/t) a_t D[t-1,r-1]`, with D[t,0]=1.

Complementation allows q=min(k,n-k) while retaining the original k in the exponent denominator. Directed actual-weight arguments, adjacent-value exp/log enclosures and positive recurrence coefficients yield a moment upper. Arithmetic cost O(n²q), working storage O(n+q). The exhaustive integer-proxy version and its weight-drift allowance remain an independent reference; the default no longer needs that allowance. Actual finite constants differ slightly rather than being falsely called byte-identical.

This is a self-contained specialization of established symmetric-convex range ideas, with a standard symmetric-mean recurrence. [Xiang (2006), UTEP-CS-06-33](https://scholarworks.utep.edu/cs_techrep/200/) predates the application. The public primary abstract was inspected; its full report download returned 403. The general theorem is not claimed to have been independently reconstructed from that abstract; the proof for these shared-lower-endpoint boxes is supplied above and in the manuscript. No new general algorithm is claimed.

## Weight-sensitive product and harmonic minimum

Uniform-subset inclusion gives P(S⊂A)=(k)_{|S|}/(n)_{|S|}≤(k/n)^{|S|}. Expanding products of nonnegative inclusion increments bounds their expectation by the corresponding independent Bernoulli product. Complementary inclusion indicators give the negative-sign comparison. This is the established negative-association principle, attributed to [Joag-Dev and Proschan (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176346079); the original full article retrieval was blocked, so the application includes its own positive-expansion proof rather than claiming inspection of that full text.

With p=k/n, convex endpoint reduction yields

`B_P=max_s sum_j log[(1-p) exp(-s λ W_j)+p exp(s λ(n-k)W_j/k)]`.

At a half quota this is sum log cosh(λW_j). The per-block minimum with the established harmonic quadratic bound is still a bound on the same moment, chosen from baseline quantities. It is not an outcome-selected confidence-event intersection. Baseline scalar search and directed arithmetic are separate: an approximate optimizer selects a valid fixed λ, then directed arithmetic encloses its moment/radius. The same search bounds and six initial scales are used across the designed moment methods.

## Restricted empirical-Bernstein adaptation

[Waudby-Smith and Ramdas (2020), Theorem 3.2 and Appendix B.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.04347) supplies the established WoR process. Normalize X_j=W_jy_j/Wmax and fix a bounded forecast γ=sum(W)/(2nWmax). Predictable scales θ_t=λ(n/k)Wmax(n-k)/(n-t)<1 telescope the sum to the centered HT residual. Compensation uses `ρ(θ)=-log(1-θ)-θ` times the realized squared prediction residual. The source's conditional elementary argument permits this fixed predictable bounded forecast. Both signs have expectation at most one. This is an adaptation with observed compensation, not a new theorem or the full adaptive betting family.

For fixed forecasts each observed log contribution is concave in its score, so a reporting-interval endpoint minimizes it. Directed endpoint minima give a pathwise lower test without ignorable reporting. Independent blocks multiply the averages; population factors cancel at the global weighted mean. Observable affine tests use fixed total weight N, unlike the original quota procedure's centered ratio representation using its realized HT denominator. The baseline uniform-score variance heuristic selects λ only; it is not an assumption on true scores or a certified moment proxy.
