## Interpretation and review status

HDDS measures household food access, not individual nutritional adequacy. Lower/large cash is the fitted mean winner; the observed-sample fitted gain over Gikuriro is 0.237 groups. The exploratory block region's 0.570-group bound fails synthetic coverage (695/800 equal weights, 592/800 unequal weights) and remains unvalidated. Support/costs alone give 9.600 groups, deterministic consistency 8.073764, and the earlier finite coherent intersection 7.720305. The quota event's 5.868/0.503 remains broad. Its regional lower bound does not imply no better valid inference could succeed.

All guarantees condition on fixed released weights, uniform conditional quotas, independent blocks and stable potential outcomes. Wider-frame representation, deployment transport, changed participation/spillovers and mixed-rollout marginal activation costs require additional evidence. The budget controls expected unit expenditure, not a hard realized cap. Hypothetical institutional classes, unused-fund values and fixed-outcome cost scenarios retain their earlier-region labels. Own-menu loss can shrink by changing its comparator; this is not a true dietary opportunity-cost estimate.
